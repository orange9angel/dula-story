# Open Japanese voice references

These files are local evaluation references for cross-language F5-TTS tests.
They are not installed into the episode dialogue unless a test is explicitly
approved.

## Female — Tsukuyomi-chan Corpus Vol. 1

- Official source: https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/corpus/
- Selected file: `VOICEACTRESS100_001.wav`
- Voice/CV: Rei Yumesaki
- Intended style: high-pitched, whispery, anime-character-like voice around
  age 14.
- Terms: use for voice synthesis and for voicing other characters is allowed,
  including commercial projects, subject to the official restrictions and
  required credit.
- Required credit for this test:

  `つくよみちゃんコーパス（CV.夢前黎）`
  `https://tyc.rei-yumesaki.net/material/corpus/`

## Male — JVS corpus, speaker jvs003

- Official source:
  https://sites.google.com/site/shinnosuketakamichi/research-topics/jvs_corpus
- Selected official sample: `jvs003_VOICEACTRESS100_001.wav`
- Corpus: Japanese Versatile Speech (JVS), professional adult speakers.
- Terms: academic research, non-commercial research, and personal use are
  allowed. Commercial use requires separate permission. Redistribution of the
  corpus is not permitted.

## Shared reference transcript

`また、東寺のように、五大明王と呼ばれる、主要な明王の中央に配されることも多い。`

The reference audio and reference transcript must remain complete and aligned;
truncating the final words can make F5-TTS prepend the missing suffix to every
generated line.
